# Decision log — Close Duty (operator-side closure)

Run: `ux-bluestar-2026-08-23` · Facilitator: BMad UX · User: Yash

## Intake

Carried in from four exchanges of live investigation rather than elicited fresh.
Verified against the production database (`pmuppvvhcafrgfysubtc`), not against
`supabase/migrations/`, which drifts.

- **27 of 31 drivers have no `auth_user_id`.** They cannot open the driver app.
  The driver-app close path (`driver_close_duty`) is unreachable for 87% of the
  fleet. Operator close is the *primary* capture path, not a fallback.
- `closed_at`, `end_odo`, `signature_path` have exactly one writer:
  `driver_close_duty`, gated on `current_driver_id()`. No operator path exists.
- `closed_at` is a money field. `computeAllowances` returns 0 overtime when it is
  null (`src/lib/allowances.ts:98`); `timeTotals` and `kmTotals` return null
  without their pairs, so the duty slip prints "—" and invoices at base rate.
- Production backlog is small: 3 duties with `closed_at IS NULL` but reading as
  Completed. The structural gap is the 27 drivers.

## Decisions

### D1 — The operator is transcribing, not reviewing. *(Yash, framing)*

> "If the driver fails to add certain things while completing his duty, or rather
> does not even start the duty, the fallback option is for the operator to get all
> the details from him and add those himself and close the duty."

The screen is a **proxy-entry surface**: an operator on the phone to a driver, or
reading a paper slip. Not a billing dashboard. Consequences:

- Field order follows the order a driver narrates a trip, not the order the
  database stores it.
- The driver's phone number belongs on the screen — they are being called *right now*.
- Photographs and signatures are unobtainable over a phone call. Their absence is
  expected, not an error state, and is what marks the record as operator-sourced.

### D2 — Surface: a dedicated modal from the row action menu. *(Yash)*

Not an extension of `DutySlipDrawer` (680px, loads allowances and expenses on
open — too heavy for entry that runs many times a day). Rejected alternative
logged: the slip drawer remains the home for FR-56 correction of an
already-closed duty.

**Open**: house convention puts edit forms in `Drawer` and reserves modals for
confirmation. This is specced as a centred modal per Yash's pick; revisit if it
reads inconsistent beside `AddBookingDrawer` and `AllotDrawer`.

### D3 — FR-59's time gate is dropped. *(Yash, departure from PRD)*

FR-59: *"Once a duty's scheduled end has passed and it is still `On-Going`, the
operator can close it themselves. Before that point they cannot — an in-progress
duty belongs to the driver."*

Yash chose **no gate**: Close Duty is available on any `On-Going` duty.

Rationale: FR-59 assumed drivers use the app. For 27 of 31 drivers the duty never
belongs to a driver in any operational sense, so the gate would make the operator
wait on duties nobody else could ever close.

**Risk accepted**: an operator can close a duty out from under a driver who is
mid-trip and about to submit their own reading. Mitigated in design, not by a
block — when the duty's driver *has* a login and now falls inside the duty
window, the modal carries an inline notice naming the driver. See EXPERIENCE.md
§ State Patterns.

### D4 — Driver-captured figures are read-only; changing one is a separate act. *(Yash)*

Anything the driver captured shows locked, beside its evidence. Editing it is
FR-56 correction — a distinct, attributed action writing `corrected_by` /
`corrected_at`. Closing and overriding-the-driver must not blur into one gesture,
or the signature stops being the authority FR-57 says it is.

### D5 — Readings are optional, and "not known" is a checkbox. *(PRD, FR-59/FR-65)*

FR-59 allows closing with no readings; FR-65 makes such a duty invoice at base
rate. An empty odometer field cannot distinguish "haven't typed it yet" from
"the driver does not know it". A ticked box is a recorded fact. When ticked the
modal states the consequence plainly — no extra km, no extra hours.

Supersedes the facilitator's earlier draft, which made the odometer pair
mandatory.

### D6 — Timestamps default to the planned window. Never `now()`.

`started_at` defaults to `start_date + reporting_time`; `closed_at` to
`end_date + est_drop_time`. Defaults are labelled as coming from the booking.

This is the fix for the production duty that computed ~550 hours of overtime: due
to drop 25 Jul 2026, closed 17 Aug 2026. Defaulting to the planned window makes
overtime compute to exactly 0 — the neutral result. Overtime is only ever created
by an operator deliberately typing a later time.

### D7 — No new RPC, no migration.

Operator RLS on `duties` is a single `ALL` policy (`booking_id in (select id from
bookings)`). The `duties_odo_forward` constraint already rejects a backwards
reading. `closed_by_profile`, `corrected_by`, `corrected_at` have existed unused
since migration 025. A plain `.update()` covers it.

## Conflicts surfaced

**FR-54 is violated in production.** It requires that a duty "is not
auto-`Completed` because its end date passed if the driver never closed it".
Migration 026's `duties_status.effective_status`, written after the PRD, does
exactly that. This is why a back-dated allotment reads Completed with an empty
slip, and why the rows needing closure are the ones that look finished.

Not resolved here — flagged for the implementation phase. Whether the view
changes or FR-54 is retired is an architecture decision, not a UX one.

**FR-58 and FR-59 must ship together.** Per the addendum: FR-59 removes the only
existing mechanism for closing a forgotten duty, so without FR-58's list "duties
will silently stop being invoiced". The "Needs closing" filter is not a
nice-to-have alongside this modal — it is the other half of it.
